Data Deletion

Last updated: 15 August 2026

1. What this page is for

If you have messaged the De Jure Academy Facebook Page and interacted with our automated Messenger assistant, you can ask us to delete the information we hold about you. This page explains what we hold, how to request deletion, and how to check on a request.

2. What we delete

DataWhen it is deleted
Your conversation history and chat context held by the assistant Immediately and automatically when we receive the request
Your Messenger profile ID and saved name in our assistant's records Immediately and automatically when we receive the request
Any name and phone number you shared for enrollment follow-up, held by our admissions team Removed by our staff within 30 days of the request

Messages stored by Meta inside Messenger itself are controlled by Meta, not by us. To remove those, use the delete options inside the Messenger app or Meta's own tools.

3. Option A — remove the app from Facebook

This is the fastest route and needs no email:

  1. On Facebook, go to Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites.
  2. Find SOJAG AI in the list and choose Remove.
  3. Tick the option to delete the activity and posts the app has on your account.

Facebook then notifies us automatically, we delete your data straight away, and Facebook shows you a confirmation code. Keep that code — you can check the status of your request at any time at:

https://bot.dejureacademy.net/data-deletion/status?code=YOUR_CODE

4. Option B — email us

Send an email to support@dejureacademy.com with the subject line “Data deletion request”, and include:

  • The name you use on Facebook, so we can locate your conversation.
  • The phone number you shared with us, if you shared one.

We will confirm by reply and complete the deletion within 30 days.

5. Checking on a request

If you have a confirmation code from Option A, open the status link in Section 3 with your code in place of YOUR_CODE. The page will show when the request was received and what was removed. For email requests, simply reply to our confirmation message.
